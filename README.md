## 🧠 What is Reinforcement Learning?

If you're new to AI, reinforcement learning might sound complex, but here's a simple way to think about it: it's like teaching a duck to find breadcrumbs. The duck starts with no idea what to do. Through trial and error, it tries different actions, gets rewards for good behavior, and gradually learns the best strategy to collect as many crumbs as possible.

In microduck-lab, the same principle applies. Your Pollen Microduck starts blank, then through your guidance and the training algorithms, it learns how to navigate, avoid obstacles, and achieve goals. The best part? You can watch every step of the learning process live in your browser.
